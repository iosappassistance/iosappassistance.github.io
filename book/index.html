Frequently asked questions

About Book and how it works.

Which formats does Book open?

Nine formats: EPUB, FB2, PDF, TXT, MOBI, AZW3, RTF, DOCX and CBZ comics. Add books from the Files app, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, Mail, Safari or any app with Share — no conversion needed.

Are my books uploaded anywhere?

No. Your books, highlights, notes and reading stats are stored only on your device and open offline. There is no account.

Can Book read aloud?

Yes. Text to speech reads text-based books aloud, so you can keep going while you cook, commute or rest your eyes.

Is Book free?

Book is free to download. Book Pro unlocks all 9 formats, read aloud, full stats and goals, and unlimited books and shelves — as a monthly or yearly subscription (yearly includes a 7-day free trial) or a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. See the Terms of Use.
