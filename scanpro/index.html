Frequently asked questions

About scanning, filling and signing PDFs with ScanPro.

How do I scan an ID card to PDF on iPhone?

Open ScanPro, choose ID card, place the card in the frame and capture. The app detects the edges and saves a clean PDF you can share or print.

How do I fill in and sign a PDF form on iPhone?

Open the PDF in ScanPro, tap to type text, tick boxes and add dates, then sign with a saved signature or draw one with your finger. Export the signed PDF and send it.

Can I scan a document or photo to PDF?

Yes. Point the camera at the paper, ScanPro finds the edges, crops the page and saves it as a PDF. You can also convert photos and images already in your library.

Can ScanPro merge or compress PDF files?

Yes. Merge several PDFs into one, split and reorder pages, and compress a file to send it by email or upload it to a form.

Does ScanPro read text from a scan (OCR)?

Yes. OCR pulls text out of a scan, including handwriting, so you can copy it.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Your files stay on your device.

Is ScanPro free?

The core scanner is free to download. ScanPro Pro unlocks unlimited scans, OCR, signing, merge, compress and export, as a subscription (weekly, monthly, annual) or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.
