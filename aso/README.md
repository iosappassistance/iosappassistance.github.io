DriveGE is a modern app to prepare for the theory driving licence exam in Georgia. Official exam questions, convenient practice and clear explanations for every question — everything you need to pass on your first try.

• Official question base. The tickets follow the theory exam question bank published by Georgia's Service Agency (SA) — no made-up answers.

• Mistake review: the app remembers where you go wrong and helps close your weak spots.
• Favourite questions and quick search across rules and road signs.

Preparation for the driving licence categories used in Georgia (A, B, C, D, AM and more).

DriveGE PRO
