Privacy Policy

Cyli · Effective 13 November 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Cyli app for Apple devices, including its Apple Watch app and widgets, together with any related services operated by IE Artem Dorozhkin (the "Service Provider") (collectively, the "Application").

Your Health Data Never Leaves Your Device

This is the most important thing to know about the Application. Everything you log, including periods, symptoms, mood, sleep, medications, lab results, notes and questions for your doctor, is stored only on your device and protected by iOS data protection. Your health data is never uploaded to the Service Provider's servers or to any third party, never used for advertising, marketing, data mining or training any model, and never seen by the Service Provider. There is no account and no login in the Application.

Forecasts and patterns are calculated on your device and work without an internet connection.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, the Application reads and writes only the data types you allow, such as menstrual flow, symptoms, sleep, heart rate, temperature and weight. Data from Apple Health is used solely to provide the Application's features. It is never shared with third parties, never used for advertising and never stored in iCloud. You can change or revoke access at any time in the Health app.

Notifications and Face ID

Medication and cycle reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your device. App lock uses Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode through iOS, and the Application never receives biometric data. These permissions are used solely for the actions you initiate, and you can review or revoke them at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security.

Reports and Backups

A PDF report for your doctor, a backup and a CSV export are created only when you ask for them. Files are saved wherever you send them yourself, such as Files, your doctor or another app. The Service Provider never receives them. You control the data in any file you share.

What Information Is Collected

The Application has no registration or login and does not ask you for personal information such as your name, email address or phone number. It contains no advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics SDKs, and does not send usage analytics to the Service Provider.

The Application does not display advertising, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. For this reason no App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown.

Purchases

Cyli Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through your Apple Account. The Service Provider never receives your payment details. The Application verifies purchases directly with Apple through StoreKit and uses no third-party purchase services. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Location

The Application does not request or collect location information.

Your Choices

You may manage or cancel a subscription at any time in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. The "Delete all data" option in the Application's Settings immediately erases all data from your device. Data written to Apple Health is deleted in the Health app. Deleting the Application removes its data from your device. If you have questions about your data, or wish to request deletion of any information you have voluntarily sent to the Service Provider (for example by email), contact iosappassistance@gmail.com.

Children

The Application is not directed to children under 13 years of age, or such higher age as required by applicable law. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact the Service Provider so that the necessary action can be taken.

Security

Because your data is stored only on your device under iOS encryption and no personal data is collected directly, the risk of exposure is minimal. No system is completely secure; if a data breach occurs involving information you have voluntarily provided, the Service Provider will notify you as required by applicable law.

Changes

The Service Provider may update this Privacy Policy from time to time and will post material changes on this page and in the Application with a new effective date. Previous versions are available on request at iosappassistance@gmail.com.

Your Consent

By using the Application you consent to the processing described in this Privacy Policy, including the processing of health data on your device. You may withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your data or the Application, without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal.

Contact

Questions about privacy: iosappassistance@gmail.com.
