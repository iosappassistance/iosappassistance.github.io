Frequently asked questions

About Pixelate and how it works.

How do I blur a face in a photo on iPhone?

Open the photo in Pixelate and the app finds every face automatically. Choose blur, pixelate or a solid box, adjust the strength and export. You can also paint over any area by hand.

Does Pixelate upload my photos?

No. Every photo is processed on your device, face detection uses Apple's on-device frameworks, and the tools work offline. Nothing is uploaded.

Can I hide numbers and text on an ID, passport or document?

Yes. Select the area you want to hide (ID numbers, card numbers, addresses, licence plates) on a photo or screenshot and cover it with blur, mosaic or a solid box.

Can I censor a screenshot?

Yes. Open the screenshot, mark the sensitive parts and export a copy (image or PDF) with the data hidden. The original is not changed.

Is Pixelate free?

Pixelate is free to download. Some tools need Pixelate Pro, available as a subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.
