Privacy Policy

Hide Faces & Data · Pixelate · Effective 27 July 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Hide Faces & Data - Pixelate app for Apple devices, together with any related services operated by IE Artem Dorozhkin (the "Service Provider") (collectively, the "Application").

Your Photos Never Leave Your Device

This is the most important thing to know about the Application. Every photo you open, edit, pixelate, blur or export is processed entirely on your device. Your images are never uploaded to the Service Provider's servers or to any third party, never stored remotely, never used to train any model, and never seen by the Service Provider or anyone else. There is no account, no login and no cloud sync in the Application.

The Application works without an internet connection for its core editing functions.

Face and Text Detection

The Application can find faces and text automatically so that you can hide them in one tap, and it also lets you select areas manually. All detection runs locally on your device using Apple's on-device frameworks. Detection determines only where a face or a piece of text appears in an image — it does not identify who a person is.

No biometric identifiers are created, stored, shared or transmitted. The Application does not build face templates or faceprints, does not perform facial recognition, and does not match faces against any database. Detection results exist only in memory while you are editing and are discarded afterwards.

Photo Library and Camera Access

The Application asks for access to your photo library so that you can choose images to edit and save the edited result back. If you use the camera, access is requested for capturing a new photo. These permissions are used solely for the editing you initiate. You can review or revoke them at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security. Access is limited to the photos you select.

What Information Is Collected

The Application has no registration or login and does not ask you for personal information such as your name, email address or phone number. Two third-party services collect a limited amount of technical data:

  • Usage analytics — anonymous events describing how the Application is used (for example, which features are opened and whether an export succeeded), along with device type, operating system version, app version, coarse region and diagnostic data such as crash reports. This data is not linked to your identity and never contains your photos or their contents.
  • Purchase data — your subscription status, purchase history and an app-specific identifier, used to unlock and restore Pixelate Pro across your devices.

The Application does not display advertising, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. For this reason no App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown.

Which Third Parties Receive This Data

The Application relies on the following providers, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Amplitude (product analytics) — receives the anonymous usage and diagnostic events described above. See the Amplitude Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat (subscription management) — receives purchase history and an app-specific user identifier to manage and restore your purchases. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Apple — processes all payments through your Apple Account. The Service Provider never receives your payment details.

Neither of these providers receives your photos, because your photos never leave your device.

Location

The Application does not request or collect location information. Photos you select may contain location metadata recorded by your camera; that metadata stays inside the image file on your device and is never transmitted to the Service Provider.

Your Choices

You can stop all data collection by uninstalling the Application. You may manage or cancel a subscription at any time in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Because analytics data is not linked to your identity, it generally cannot be traced back to an individual user; if you have questions about data associated with your purchases, or wish to request deletion of any information you have voluntarily sent to the Service Provider, contact iosappassistance@gmail.com.

Children

The Application is not directed to children under 13 years of age, or such higher age as required by applicable law. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact the Service Provider so that the necessary action can be taken.

Security

Because your photos are processed only on your device and no personal data is collected directly, the risk of exposure is minimal. Data held by the providers listed above is protected by their own security measures. No system is completely secure; if a data breach occurs involving information you have voluntarily provided, the Service Provider will notify you as required by applicable law.

Changes

The Service Provider may update this Privacy Policy from time to time and will post material changes on this page with a new effective date. Previous versions are available on request at iosappassistance@gmail.com.

Your Consent

By using the Application you consent to the processing described in this Privacy Policy. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal.

Contact

Questions about privacy: iosappassistance@gmail.com.
