Privacy Policy

Tesela · Effective 13 November 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Tesela app for Apple devices, together with any related services operated by IE Artem Dorozhkin (the "Service Provider") (collectively, the "Application").

Your Videos Never Leave Your Device

This is the most important thing to know about the Application. Every video you open, scan, edit and export is processed entirely on your device. Your videos are never uploaded to the Service Provider's servers or to any third party, never stored remotely, never used to train any model, and never seen by the Service Provider or anyone else. There is no account, no login and no cloud sync in the Application.

Finding, tracking and hiding objects in a video works without an internet connection.

Detection of Faces, Plates, Documents and Text

The Application can find faces, licence plates, documents, barcodes and text that looks like personal data (such as phone numbers, email addresses or card numbers) so that you can hide them, and it also lets you mark areas by hand. All detection and tracking runs locally on your device using Apple's on-device frameworks and machine-learning models bundled with the Application. Detection determines only where an object appears in a frame — it does not identify who a person is, and recognised text is used only to decide whether an area should be hidden.

No biometric identifiers are created, stored, shared or transmitted. The Application does not build face templates or faceprints, does not perform facial recognition, and does not match faces against any database. Detection results are stored only inside the project on your device and are deleted together with the project.

Photo Library, Camera, Microphone and Notifications

You choose videos through the system picker, which gives the Application access only to the videos you select. The Application asks for permission to add to your photo library when you save an exported video. If you record a new video inside the Application, it asks for access to the camera and the microphone for that recording. The Application may ask to send a local notification when an export finishes in the background. These permissions are used solely for the actions you initiate, and you can review or revoke them at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security.

Exported Files

When you export a video, the Application writes a new file and leaves the original unchanged. Location and other capture metadata of the original are not copied into the exported file.

What Information Is Collected

The Application has no registration or login and does not ask you for personal information such as your name, email address or phone number. It contains no advertising SDKs and does not send usage analytics to the Service Provider.

The Application does not display advertising, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. For this reason no App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown.

Purchases

Tesela Pro purchases and the optional subscription are processed by Apple through your Apple Account. The Service Provider never receives your payment details. To verify purchases and unlock Pro features, the Application uses RevenueCat, a subscription-management service. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous identifier generated by the Application, your purchase and subscription status from Apple, and basic technical information such as app version, device model, operating-system version, language and country. This information is not linked to your name, email address or videos. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

Location

The Application does not request or collect location information. Videos you select may contain location metadata recorded by your camera; that metadata is never transmitted to the Service Provider.

Your Choices

You may manage or cancel a subscription at any time in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Deleting the Application removes its projects from your device. If you have questions about data associated with your purchases, or wish to request deletion of the anonymous purchase record or of any information you have voluntarily sent to the Service Provider (for example by email), contact iosappassistance@gmail.com.

Children

The Application is not directed to children under 13 years of age, or such higher age as required by applicable law. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact the Service Provider so that the necessary action can be taken.

Security

Because your videos are processed only on your device and no personal data is collected directly, the risk of exposure is minimal. Data held by the providers listed above is protected by their own security measures. No system is completely secure; if a data breach occurs involving information you have voluntarily provided, the Service Provider will notify you as required by applicable law.

Changes

The Service Provider may update this Privacy Policy from time to time and will post material changes on this page with a new effective date. Previous versions are available on request at iosappassistance@gmail.com.

Your Consent

By using the Application you consent to the processing described in this Privacy Policy. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal.

Contact

Questions about privacy: iosappassistance@gmail.com.
