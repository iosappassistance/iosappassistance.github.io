Frequently asked questions

About Cleaner and how it works.

How does Cleaner free up space on my iPhone?

Cleaner scans your photo library on device and groups the things that usually waste the most space: near-duplicate photos, old screenshots, large videos and the hidden video part of Live Photos. You review each group and delete only what you choose — nothing is ever removed automatically.

Does Cleaner upload my photos or contacts anywhere?

No. Cleaner has no server and makes no network requests with your content. All analysis runs on your device. Your photos, videos, contacts and notes never leave your iPhone.

What is Secret Space?

A passcode-protected vault inside Cleaner for private photos, videos, contacts and notes. It locks automatically when you leave the app and can be unlocked with Face ID.

Can Cleaner really eject water from my speaker?

The speaker tool plays low-frequency tones that vibrate the speaker membrane, which can help push out water droplets and dust — the same idea Apple Watch uses. Results vary and it is not a repair service.

Is Cleaner free?

Yes. Cleaner is free to download and use on the App Store.
