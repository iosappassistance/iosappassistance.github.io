Frequently asked questions

About reading, editing, signing and converting PDFs with PDF.

How do I sign a PDF on iPhone?

Open the PDF, add your signature and place it on the page. Then share the signed document right away.

Can I edit a PDF on my iPhone?

You can add text, highlight content, draw and mark up pages.

Can I scan a document and extract the text?

Yes. Scan with the camera and the OCR scanner extracts text from scanned PDFs.

How do I convert a PDF to Word, Excel or JPG?

Use the converter to export a PDF to Word, Excel, JPG and other formats.

Can I password-protect a PDF?
